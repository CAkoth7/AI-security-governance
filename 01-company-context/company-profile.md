# Laurent Technologies — Company Profile

## 1. Company Overview

Laurent Technologies is a fictional financial technology organization providing digital financial services to individual and small-business customers.

The organization operates primarily through digital channels and relies on cloud-based technology, data analytics, artificial intelligence, application programming interfaces (APIs), and third-party technology providers to deliver and support its services.

Laurent has approximately 1,500 employees and operates across European and selected international markets.

---

## 2. Core Business Services

Laurent Technologies provides the following services:

### Digital Lending

The organization provides digital lending services to individual and small-business customers. AI and data analytics are used to support aspects of credit assessment and lending operations.

### Payments

Laurent provides digital payment services and processes customer transactions through its digital platforms.

### Fraud Detection

Machine-learning models are used to identify potentially fraudulent transactions and suspicious customer activity.

### Customer Support

Laurent uses AI-enabled customer-service capabilities to support customers through digital channels.

### Marketing and Personalization

Data analytics and AI are used to personalize selected customer communications, product recommendations, and marketing activities.

---

## 3. Technology Environment

Laurent operates a predominantly cloud-based technology environment.

The environment includes:

* Cloud infrastructure
* Customer-facing web and mobile applications
* APIs and integration services
* Centralized data platforms
* Machine-learning models
* Generative AI applications
* Identity and access management systems
* Security monitoring capabilities
* Third-party SaaS and AI services

AI systems may be developed internally, procured from third-party providers, or embedded within existing technology platforms.

---

## 4. Data Environment

Laurent processes several categories of information as part of its business operations, including:

* Customer identification information
* Financial information
* Transaction data
* Account information
* Customer interaction data
* Employee information
* Operational data
* Model and system telemetry

Some AI systems may process personal or otherwise sensitive information depending on their purpose and implementation.

## 6. Organizational Accountability

AI systems may involve multiple stakeholders, including:

* Business owners
* Technology owners
* Data owners
* Information security
* Risk management
* Legal and compliance
* Data protection
* Internal audit
* Senior management

Accountability for an AI system is therefore not limited to the technical team responsible for implementing the system.

---

## 7. Purpose of the AI Inventory

As AI adoption increases, Laurent requires a structured mechanism for identifying and documenting AI systems across the organization.

The AI inventory developed in this project is intended to provide visibility into:

* AI systems in use or under development
* Business purpose
* System ownership
* Data processed
* Affected individuals
* Level of automation
* Human oversight
* Third-party dependencies
* Regulatory considerations
* Enterprise risk
* Governance and review requirements

The inventory will serve as the foundation for subsequent AI risk assessment, classification, control selection, and ongoing monitoring.
